Rafał Sionek

Skiing is more than a sport. It is a way to experience the mountains.

Professional ISIA snow sports instructor, ski guide, alpine and cross-country ski instructor, and ski touring enthusiast.

For more than 20 years I have worked in the mountains, coaching skiers, leading trips and developing my own experience in varied snow and terrain conditions. During that time I have skied and worked in many regions across six continents.

My coaching focuses on conscious technique, safety and a pace tailored to each individual. There is no single formula for everyone — only strong fundamentals, consistency and plenty of practice.
