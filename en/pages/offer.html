How I work

Coaching tailored to you — not the other way around.

There is no single model of good skiing that works for everyone. The starting point is your level, goal, fitness and what you genuinely want to learn.

We can work on the fundamentals, performance technique, smoother skiing, preparation for more difficult terrain or simply greater confidence on skis.
